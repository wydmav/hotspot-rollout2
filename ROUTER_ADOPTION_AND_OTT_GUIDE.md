# Router Adoption & Real OTT Voucher Testing Guide

This guide takes you step-by-step through:
1. **Adopting a physical MikroTik router** (hAP ax³, hAP ac³, or any RouterOS v6/v7 device) into the T-Connect Control Tower.
2. **Configuring OTT Voucher Lesotho** with your official merchant portal credentials (`portal.ottlesotho.com`).
3. **Understanding where vouchers are generated vs. redeemed**.
4. **Testing live OTT voucher redemption** on an adopted router through the captive portal.

---

## 1. Where Are Vouchers Generated vs. Redeemed?

### The Two Voucher Types in T-Connect:
1. **T-Connect Local Retail Vouchers (Generated by You)**:
   - **Where they live**: Under the **Billing & Vouchers** tab in the sidebar.
   - **How they work**: You click **Generate Batch**, choose a denomination (e.g., M10, M60, M280), and export a batch of 4-digit PINs (`TC-XXXX-M10`). You print or sell these at spaza shops and kiosks.
   - **Redemption**: Guests enter the code in the "Have a Voucher?" card on your captive portal.

2. **OTT Vouchers (Generated Upstream by OTT Mobile / Retailers)**:
   - **Where they are generated**: Generated **externally** by OTT Mobile / banks / retail stores (Shoprite, Pick n Pay, ATMs, banking apps). Customers buy a slip with a **12-digit numeric PIN** (e.g. `8921 3482 9011`).
   - **Where you configure your credentials**: In T-Connect under **Payment Gateways** &rarr; **OTT Voucher Lesotho**.
   - **Where redemption happens**:
     - On the captive portal when a guest clicks **OTT Voucher** and enters their 12-digit PIN.
     - In the **Payment Gateways** tab &rarr; **Step 4: Sandbox & Live Redemption Lab** for instant testing.
   - **Liquidation**: The T-Connect backend receives the 12-digit PIN, contacts `portal.ottlesotho.com` via API using your `TCONNECTLES1` credentials, and liquidates the value into your merchant settlement account while granting the user immediate Wi-Fi access.

## 2. Setting Up OTT Voucher Credentials

Referencing your merchant portal screen (`portal.ottlesotho.com/APISettings`):

1. **API USERNAME**: 
   - Pre-assigned by OTT as: `TCONNECTLES1`
2. **API KEY**:
   - In the OTT portal, click **Get New Key**.
   - Copy the alphanumeric key.
3. **API PASSWORD**:
   - In the OTT portal, click **Get New Password**.
   - Copy the secret password.
4. **Inputting into T-Connect**:
   - Go to **Payment Gateways** (or click the Payment Gateways card in Billing).
   - Click on the **OTT Voucher Lesotho** card.
   - In **Step 1: Merchant Credentials**:
     - Partner ID / Username: `TCONNECTLES1`
     - Paste your **API Key** into the API Key input.
     - Paste your **API Password** into the API Password input.
     - Click **Save Credentials & Continue to Connection Test**.
   - In **Step 2: Connection Test**:
     - Click **Run Live Connection Ping**.
     - T-Connect verifies communication with `portal.ottlesotho.com`.
   - In **Step 3: Webhook Endpoint**:
     - Webhook URL: `https://app.tconnect.co.ls/api/webhooks/ottvoucher`
   - In **Step 4: Live Redemption Lab**:
     - You can test PIN redemption with any real or sample PIN.

## 3. How to Adopt a Router Step-by-Step

### Prerequisites
- A physical MikroTik router connected to the internet (via Starlink, fiber, or LTE).
- Default IP access to the router (WebFig on `192.168.88.1` or via WinBox).

### Step 3.1: Register the Router on T-Connect
1. Navigate to **Fleet / Routers & Adoption** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **+ Add Router to Fleet** button in the top right.
3. Fill in:
   - **Router Name**: (e.g., `MikroTik-Main-Hotspot`)
   - **Vertical Target**: Select your target (`☕ Connected Hotspots`, `🏡 Villages`, `🚌 Buses`, `🏟️ Stadiums`, `🌳 Parks`).
   - **Physical Site Location**: (e.g., `Maseru Central Hub`)
   - **Hardware Model**: Select your model (e.g., `MikroTik hAP ax³ (v7)`).
   - **RouterOS Version**: `v7` (recommended) or `v6`.
   - **MAC Address**: Your router's primary MAC address (found on the router sticker or `/interface ethernet print`).
4. Click **Create Router & Generate One-Liner**.

### Step 3.2: Execute the One-Line Adoption Script
1. T-Connect displays the encrypted single-use adoption command:
   ```routeros
   :do { /tool fetch url="https://app.tconnect.co.ls/api/devices/rt_xxxx/adopt.rsc?token=adopt_tok_xxxx" http-method=post http-data="fw=7.15.2" dst-path="tc-xxxx.rsc" check-certificate=no; /import file-name="tc-xxxx.rsc" verbose=yes; /file remove "tc-xxxx.rsc"; } on-error={};
   ```
2. Open **WinBox** or **WebFig** &rarr; open **New Terminal**.
3. **Paste and press Enter**.
4. What happens automatically:
   - Secure WireGuard tunnel is configured (`10.99.0.0/16`).
   - FreeRADIUS AAA is bound to hotspot profiles (`port 1812/1813 UDP`).
   - Walled Garden exceptions are automatically installed for OTT Voucher (`portal.ottlesotho.com`, `api.ottvoucher.com`, `ottlesotho.com`), ensuring captive guests can validate PINs before gaining full internet access.
   - 15-second deadman watchdog heartbeat starts.
5. In T-Connect, the router status badge turns **ONLINE (GREEN)** within 15 seconds.

## 4. Testing Live OTT Voucher Redemption on the Adopted Router

1. **Connect a Phone or Laptop** to the router's Wi-Fi network (SSID: `T-Connect_Hotspot` or your bridge).
2. The device triggers the captive portal automatically (via RFC 8910 captive portal detection).
3. On the captive portal:
   - Select a plan (e.g., Daily Pass - M10).
   - Under Payment Options, select **OTT Voucher**.
   - Enter your **12-digit OTT Voucher PIN**.
   - Click **Redeem OTT Voucher & Connect**.
4. **What happens behind the scenes**:
   - The T-Connect backend atomically creates a `pending` transaction.
   - Liquidates the PIN with `portal.ottlesotho.com`.
   - Atomically marks the transaction `completed` and issues an active access entitlement.
   - FreeRADIUS issues an immediate RADIUS Access-Accept to the MikroTik hotspot.
   - The user device is unblocked and connected to the Internet.
5. In T-Connect:
   - The transaction appears in **Billing & Vouchers &rarr; Transactions**.
   - The session appears under **Live Hotspot Sessions**.
   - An audit trail entry records the redemption.
